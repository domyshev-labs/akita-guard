связывают её с управлением доступом. Непрозрачный фон, без текста и рамки.

## Финальный промпт

Use case: logo-brand. Create one final square 1024x1024 opaque iOS app icon for Akita Guard, a personal home-network access management app. Full-bleed square canvas; do NOT draw rounded outer corners or an app mockup. Large centered friendly but alert Japanese Akita dog head with triangular upright ears, broad cream cheeks and muzzle, warm ginger fur, dark expressive simple eyes and nose. Beautiful crisp simplified premium app illustration, subtly sculpted forms, instantly legible at 48px, not photorealistic and not childish clipart. Deep dark forest-green background; mint green accents harmonize with an existing mint/forest green app UI. Below the chin integrate a SMALL mint-green network symbol like a collar medallion: three round nodes connected by clean thick lines. Dog face is the unmistakable primary subject; no busy circuitry or server racks. Balanced silhouette, generous safe space around ears, subject fills around 75% of canvas. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, outer border or shield obscuring the face.
